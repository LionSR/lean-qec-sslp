\section{Code catalogue of small diagonal distance-two codes}
\label{supp:code-catalog}

Our multi-agent system performed large-scale parameter sweeps on a local high-performance computing workstation equipped with an Intel Xeon w7-3565X CPU and two Nvidia RTX 6000 Ada Generation GPUs. The search focused on distance-2 codes for $n \le 6$ qubits and logical dimensions $K \in \{2,3,4\}$. This procedure, executed by the Search Agent and verified by the Audit Agent, yielded a rich catalog of new nonadditive quantum codes. The following subsections present these codes, organized by logical dimension $K$. Each entry includes the code parameters, the explicit logical states with exact rational amplitudes, and the resulting transversal diagonal gate.
